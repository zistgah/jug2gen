# Foundry-in-a-Box and winding

Proposed shop bridge [Cat 4]. Not a qualified procedure.

## Foundry

Minimum picture from the notes: waste-oil or forced-draft furnace, later a small induction set; green-sand or equivalent moulds; sorting before melt.

Metals treated as realistic at this stage: aluminium, brass, bronze. Mild steel melt and Ti-6Al-4V are outside the box. Titanium needs vacuum practice to avoid oxygen and nitrogen embrittlement. [Cat 3/4]

As-cast faces are not bearing seats. The lab splits cast blanks from machined fits.

Energy in the lab is `m (c ΔT + latent) / η_furnace`. Furnace efficiency is an assumption, default well below 1.

Safety is part of the capability, not a footnote: face shield, dry charge, no wet metal, ventilation, a second person, no improvised sealed vessel.

Transition out of roadside fabrication: a repeated alloy, a mould that a second person can ram, and a measured dimension on a witness coupon.

## Winding

Regional winding means laminated cores, magnet wire, hand winding, local varnish. Standardise slot count, gauge, turns, insulation class, and termination before comparing two motors.

Expected performance is below a commercial servo on torque density and thermal margin. It is enough if the machine can be rewound locally. QA minimum: resistance, inductance if a meter exists, no-load current, direction, temperature rise on a timed load. [Cat 4/5]

Virgin copper matters because contaminated recyclate drops conductivity, raises `I²R`, and cuts MMF per slot. [Cat 3/5]

Unavoidable purchases at this stage: enamel, precision races, NdFeB if a permanent-magnet topology is chosen, semiconductor dice. SRM is the rare-earth fallback. [Cat 3/4]
