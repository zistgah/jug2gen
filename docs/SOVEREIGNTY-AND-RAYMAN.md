# Sovereignty, federation, Ray-Man

## Deterrent principle

Design threshold, not a statute: critical function replaceable locally on a bounded clock. The notes use 72 hours. [Cat 4/6]

Always local if the claim is Swaraj: frames, basic motors, basic drives, structural castings, low-level firmware.

Safe to import: passives, fasteners, generic bearings, unworked stock.

Reserve: magnet wire, copper, magnet blanks, power transistors, microcontrollers.

Sovereignty here is substitution, not autarky. A missing neodymium rotor becomes an SRM. A locked MCU becomes RISC-V. The machine degrades; it does not have to stop. [Cat 3/4]

## People and guilds

Tiers: Mistry, Winder, Integrator, Researcher. Advancement by artefact, not by a gatekept credential, is the proposed rule. University-shaped theory still exists for nonlinear control, device layout, and structural analysis. Welding, winding, sand, and troubleshooting are apprenticeship skills. [Cat 1/3/4]

Organisation: mechanic → workshop → guild → cooperative → federation. Open specifications are the guard against a cooperative becoming the only seller. [Cat 3/4]

IP split in the notes: copyright on text, defensive publication as prior art, commons rules on derivatives. "Legally destroys novelty" is jurisdiction-bound. Publication can supply prior art. It is not a universal eraser. [Cat 3]

## Humanesque placement

Humanesque is the civilisational frame. GramSheel is the community substrate. Project VIKRAM is technical capability. Jugaad-to-Genesis is the physical production trajectory inside that frame. Cultural names can change. The kernel in the notes is event dynamics, the semantic tree, material-parity economics, and the tiered guild. [Cat 2/3]

## Flying Ray-Man

Separate programme, not an automatic consequence of a wiper motor.

Tri-phibious means aerial, surface, and subsurface. Supplied assumptions: about 85 kg, 25 kg payload, ducted fans plus undulating margins, carbon-PEEK / Ti-6Al-4V, ₹85 lakh-class prototype estimate. Designed versus conceptual subsystems are distinguished in the evidence register. 85 kg sits inside the proposed trough for handling, and outside it for materials and certification. [Cat 1/4/6]

## Post-ASI

If design intelligence becomes abundant, melt points, copper section, tool wear, and geography do not. The notes treat this pathway as the open physical substrate, not as a proof that such a substrate already exists. [Cat 3]
