## Spine

Jugaad asks whether a machine can be made to work. Swaraj asks whether it can be made again. Genesis asks whether the means of making it can themselves be made. Those are regions in a capability field, not three product generations. [Cat 3]

The field is `C = [M, F, A, S, K, E, W, V, H, O]`: materials, fabrication, actuation, sensing, compute, energy, winding, verification, human skill, organisation. A community can sit at Genesis-facing compute and Jugaad mechanics at the same time. [Cat 3/4]

The physical node is `N = ⟨L, m, τ, P, V, E⟩`. Economic regime changes where workshop infrastructure changes, even when the square-cube law is smooth. [Cat 3/6]

## Material parity

Material parity is a floor, not a promise. As maturity rises, avoidable margins can be compressed toward commodity mass times price plus unavoidable process energy. The machine does not therefore cost only its atoms. [Cat 2/3]

`C_total = C_material + C_energy + C_tools + C_labour + C_process + C_testing + C_overhead + C_failure`. [Cat 3/4]
